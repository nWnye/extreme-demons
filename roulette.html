  • You get a random level and a percentage to reach on it. The first one is 1%.
  • Reach the percentage and the next level asks for one percent more: 2%, 3%, and so on.
  • You have 3 skips. A skip gives you a new level at the same percentage.
  • Clear 100% to win.
